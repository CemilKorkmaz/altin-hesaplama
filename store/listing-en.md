# Google Play store listing — English

## App name
Altın Defterim

## Short description
Track your gold portfolio, profit/loss and simulate potential sales.

## Full description
Keep track of your gold savings with Altın Defterim.

Record gram, quarter, half, full, Republic and 14/18/22K gold purchases. See your portfolio value, total cost and profit/loss using current market prices.

Features:
• Gold type, quantity, unit price and purchase date records
• Keyless live price sources with automatic refresh
• Average cost and portfolio value by gold type
• Sale simulation without real trading
• Turkish and English interface
• One-tap dark mode
• A simple experience with on-device data storage

The sale simulator does not change your portfolio. The app does not execute real trades or provide investment advice. Prices are for information only; bank and dealer prices may differ.

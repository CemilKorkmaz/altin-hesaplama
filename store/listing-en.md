## 3) Full description

```
Altın Defterim is a simple portfolio ledger that helps you keep track of your gold savings in one place.

Record your purchases of gram, quarter, half, full, Republic and 14/18/22 karat gold. See your portfolio's total value, average cost and profit or loss at a glance using current market prices.

FEATURES
• Gold type, quantity, purchase price and date records
• Live prices refreshed automatically from publicly available sources
• Average cost and portfolio value by gold type
• Total cost and profit/loss calculation
• Sale simulation that never executes a real trade
• Turkish and English interface
• One-tap dark mode
• No account required

YOUR PRIVACY
Your portfolio records are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to a server, the cloud or the developer. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics tracking and no location access. The app only connects to the internet to download current gold prices, and your portfolio details are not shared in those requests.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION
The sale screen is a simulation only; it does not change your portfolio and does not execute a real buy or sell transaction. The app is not a bank, a gold dealer or a brokerage, and it does not provide investment advice. Prices are for information only, may be delayed or inaccurate, and bank and dealer prices may differ. Without an internet connection the app clearly indicates that it is showing sample data. You are responsible for your own investment decisions.

Download Altın Defterim to keep your gold savings organised.
```

Uygulama, güncel altın fiyatlarını göstermek için internet üzerinden aşağıdaki genel erişime açık fiyat servislerine istek gönderir:

- Truncgil Finans — `https://finans.truncgil.com/today.json`
- Truncgil Finans v4 — `https://finans.truncgil.com/v4/today.json`

Teknik zorunluluk gereği bu servislerin sunucuları, standart web sunucusu günlüklerinde IP adresi, tarayıcı/istemci bilgisi ve istek zamanı gibi bağlantı verilerini işleyebilir. Bu işleme ilgili servislerin kendi gizlilik politikalarına tabidir ve geliştiricinin kontrolünde değildir.

İnternet bağlantısı yoksa veya tüm servisler yanıt vermezse uygulama, **örnek veri** kullandığını arayüzde açıkça belirtir.

## English

### 1. About this policy

This privacy policy explains how the **Altın Defterim** mobile application (package name: `com.cemilkorkmaz.altin_hesaplama`) handles user data. By downloading or using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Data controller: C&A Digital Ventures (developer of Altın Defterim). Contact: ca.digital.ventures@gmail.com

### 2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the records you enter only on your own device. This includes:

- Gold type (gram, quarter, half, full, Republic, 22/18/14 karat jewellery, etc.)
- Quantity
- Purchase date
- Purchase price
- Language preference (Turkish / English)
- Light or dark theme preference

This data is kept in the app's private local storage on your device (Android `SharedPreferences`). **It is never sent to any server, cloud service or to the developer.** The app has no user accounts, cloud backup or cross-device sync.

### 3. What we do not collect

The app does **not** collect, request or process:

- Name, surname, email address, phone number or any identifier
- Location data
- Contacts, calendar, photos, camera or microphone access
- Advertising ID
- Analytics, crash reports or usage statistics
- Payment or financial account information

The app is free and contains no in-app purchases.

### 4. Internet access and third-party services

To display current gold prices, the app makes internet requests to the following publicly available price services:

- Truncgil Finans — `https://finans.truncgil.com/today.json`
- Truncgil Finans v4 — `https://finans.truncgil.com/v4/today.json`
- Genel Para (fallback source) — `https://api.genelpara.com/json/?list=altin`

These requests only retrieve a price list. **Your portfolio records, quantities, prices and preferences are not sent.** The app sends no user ID, session or cookies to these services.

As a technical necessity, these providers' servers may process connection data such as IP address, client information and request time in standard web server logs. Such processing is governed by the providers' own privacy policies and is outside the developer's control.

If there is no internet connection, or if all services fail to respond, the app clearly labels the figures it shows as **sample data**.

### 5. Permissions

The app uses a single Android permission:

- `android.permission.INTERNET` — to download gold prices.

No camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notification permission is requested.

### 6. Data retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

- You can delete a single record inside the app.
- To remove everything, delete the records one by one or uninstall the app.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all on-device portfolio records and preferences. **Because this data is never backed up anywhere, it cannot be recovered.**

Since the developer has no server-side data, you do not need to request deletion from the developer; there is no server-side data to delete.

### 7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data at all, no data is collected from children either.

### 8. Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

Under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing.

Because the app never transmits your personal data to the developer and uses no central database, you can exercise all of these rights directly on your device: delete records in the app, change preferences, or uninstall the app. You may still contact us with any questions at ca.digital.ventures@gmail.com.

### 9. Data security

Your data is held in your device's app-private storage, protected by the operating system's app isolation. Because the app collects no data, there is no server database that could be breached. Device security (screen lock, up-to-date operating system) is your responsibility.

### 10. Not investment advice

Prices in the app are for information only and may be delayed or inaccurate. The app does not execute real buy or sell transactions; the sale screen is a simulation only and does not modify your records. Nothing in the app constitutes investment advice.

### 11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated with new versions of the app. When changes are significant, the effective date on this page is updated. The current version is always published on this page.

### 12. Contact

For questions or requests about this privacy policy: ca.digital.ventures@gmail.com
